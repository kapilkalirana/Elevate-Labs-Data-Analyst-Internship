# Task 4: Business Performance Dashboard

## Overview
This project was completed as part of the **Elevate Labs Data Analytics Internship**.

The objective of this task is to design an **interactive business dashboard** using Tableau that enables stakeholders to analyse sales performance, profitability, customer trends, and regional performance through interactive visualisations.

---

## Objective

Design an interactive dashboard for business stakeholders using Tableau.

---

## Tool Used

- Tableau Public

---

## Dataset

- **Dataset Name:** Sample Superstore
- **File Format:** CSV
- **Rows:** 9,994
- **Columns:** 21

The dataset contains information related to:
- Orders
- Sales
- Profit
- Customers
- Categories
- Regions
- Segments
- Order Dates

---

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes the following components:

- 📈 Sales Trend Over Time
- 📊 Profit by Category
- 🌍 Sales by Region
- 👥 Top 10 Customers by Sales
- 🥧 Sales by Segment
- 📌 KPI Cards
  - Total Sales
  - Total Profit
  - Total Orders
- 🎛 Interactive Filters
  - Region
  - Category
  - Segment

---

## Key Insights

- Sales increase significantly during the last quarter of the year.
- The West region recorded the highest sales.
- Technology is the most profitable product category.
- The Consumer segment contributes the largest share of total sales.
- KPI cards provide a quick summary of overall business performance.
